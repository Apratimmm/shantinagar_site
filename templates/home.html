Our Mission

Two Key Missions of Shantinagar English School

We live these two missions every day, balancing what students know with who they become.

Knowledge

To provide a challenging and stimulating learning environment where students gain strong academic foundations and practical understanding, helping them reach their full potential and apply knowledge meaningfully in everyday life.

Character

To nurture good values, responsibility, and social skills within a caring school community, so that students develop into thoughtful, respectful individuals prepared for a meaningful life.

Our Vision

Vision

Shantinagar English School aims to develop students into lifelong learners who gain, apply, and value the knowledge and skills needed to uphold local and national values while becoming thoughtful global citizens.

To support students in reaching their full potential by encouraging curious minds and the ability to think, question, and discuss ideas clearly.
To promote respect for values and tolerance towards various races and religions.
To encourage good behaviour and a strong sense of responsibility.
To offer a broad and balanced curriculum that meets National Curriculum requirements, suited to each student's age and needs, with special focus on mathematics and language.
To help students understand the world they live in and how individuals, groups, and nations depend on one another.
To ensure equal opportunities, responsibilities, and rights for every child, regardless of race, gender, background, or ability.
