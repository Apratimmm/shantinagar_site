Events

What the year felt like.

Festivals, fairs, matches and performances from recent terms — the parts of school that never appear on a report card.

Recent Highlights
Festival 12 April 2026

Spring Culture Festival

Eleven countries represented, forty-two performances, and a courtyard full of food cooked by parents. Grades 1-10 shared the same stage.

Community 30 October 2025

Harvest Fair & House Sports

The kitchen garden crop went to stalls run entirely by Grade 7. Proceeds funded three scholarships for the coming year.

Sport 19 September 2025

All-School Sports Day

Four houses, one field, and a relay final decided by a stride. Every student in the school ran at least one event.

Academics 6 February 2026

Science & Invention Fair

Sixty-eight student projects judged by visiting researchers. Two were invited to present at the county symposium.

Arts 20 November 2025

Winter Theatre Production

A student-adapted staging of The Tempest — cast, set, lighting and score built entirely in-house over nine weeks.

Tradition 16 December 2025

Carol Service by Candlelight

The oldest thing we do. The choir closes the year in the old hall, and the whole school walks out singing.
